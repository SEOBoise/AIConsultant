    Purpose and audience

    AI Consultant Wiki is an editorial reference for U.S. business owners, managers and people exploring AI consulting. The publication explains services, implementation, costs, research and evaluation. It is not a professional standards body or independent consultant-ranking agency.

    Evidence and examples

    Preference is given to original research, public statistical agencies and official technical sources. Claims retain the study population, observation period and relevant limitations. Proposed workflows, selection rubrics and hypothetical financial calculations are editorial guidance rather than measured outcomes.

    Sources are listed with plain-text URLs. The cited organizations do not endorse this publication or its recommendations.

    Featured consultant recommendation

    Dan O’Donnell is the publisher-selected featured consultant, with the supplied website https://coffeecupai.com. The description “top AI consultant” is an editorial recommendation. It does not represent an independently scored ranking, an award or an assertion of verified superiority over other providers.

    Readers should verify current services, suitability and terms directly with a provider. No client results, credentials, testimonials or awards have been invented for this profile.

    Site operation

    This edition is a static information site. It has no visitor account system, comment submission, contact form, newsletter registration or checkout. Page search runs locally in the browser. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for the operating scope.
